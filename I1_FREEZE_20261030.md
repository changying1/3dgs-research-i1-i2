# I1 No-Stereo End8k LLFF8 Freeze (2026-10-30)

This branch freezes the code and compact evidence summaries used for the
successful eight-scene NS30K-v1 validation of Innovation 1 (I1).

## Research identity

- Internal baseline: **Binocular3DGS + Gaussian Dropout**, with Binocular
  Consistency disabled (`--no-binocular-consistency`).
- I1: **Observation-Evidence Guided Structure-Stable Refinement**.
- Dataset protocol: LLFF, 3 training views per scene.
- Random seed: seed 0.
- Training length: 30,000 iterations.
- I1 schedule: iterations 1 through 8,000 inclusive.
- I1 structural-loss weight: 0.1.
- Formal evaluation nodes: 5k, 10k, 20k, and 30k.
- Scenes: Fortress, Horns, Room, Leaves, Orchids, Fern, Flower, and T-Rex.

The frozen training and evaluation reports bind the critical source files,
view manifests, PDC inputs, MiDaS metadata, and NS30K sampling tapes by
SHA-256. Large training assets are not stored in normal Git; their paths,
sizes, and hashes are recorded in `I1_ASSET_SHA256_20261030.csv`.

## Scope and limitations

These conclusions apply only to the controlled No-Stereo B0 comparison under
the frozen NS30K-v1, 3-view, seed-0 protocol. Full binocular consistency (CA)
is an external comparison method and is not an I1 training component in this
freeze.

This freeze does **not** claim that I1 exceeds full CA, exceeds all
state-of-the-art methods, or has established multi-seed statistical
significance. Further random seeds and external baselines require separate
experiments.

## Freeze-time verification

- `py_compile`: passed for all 25 selected Python entry points and modules.
- Selected CPU pytest suite: 190 passed, 10 failed.
- Six failures are duplicate pytest/unittest reports of the legacy FSGS
  exact-AST/gate-list contract. The frozen successful implementation contains
  the later `stable_evidence_conflict` diagnostics and therefore is not
  byte-for-byte identical to the older FSGS reference implementation.
- Four failures come from the legacy `run_ns30k_candidate.py` tests assuming
  that formal output and smoke directories do not yet exist. The completed
  experiment directories are intentionally retained and were not deleted or
  renamed to satisfy those tests.
- No GPU training, rendering, or metric recomputation was performed during
  this freeze.

## Git inclusion policy

The commit includes the exact critical source revisions used by the completed
runs, their launch/evaluation utilities, CPU regression tests, this identity
record, the SHA-256 inventory, and compact metric summaries. It excludes
datasets, rendered images, full report trees, sampling-tape payloads,
pretrained weights, checkpoints, point clouds, and other large binaries.
